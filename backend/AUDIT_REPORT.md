# Backend audit and repairs

Audit date: 2026-10-08. Input: the supplied backend.zip, SHA-256 `7272fe4794742b61288a9fd2af886188044e1a60936f2589fc2f05cf0cefd9f7`.

This archive is a corrected source revision, not a claim that every possible defect has been eliminated. Application services, controllers, authorization boundaries, DTOs, database schema, configuration, utility scripts, payment/verification integrations, and the corresponding app contracts were reviewed. Compilation, automated checks, dependency auditing, and targeted full-application regression tests support the changes. The compiled admin UI was inspected as an artifact; its editable source was absent and it was not rebuilt. Vendored/minified assets have not received the same semantic review as backend source.

## Material fixes

| Area | Confirmed problem | Corrected behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity | UID impersonation, missing or weak authentication boundaries, untrusted verification/profile fields | Firebase ID tokens are checked with revocation checking. Server-derived identity and verified claims control private access. Role, KYC, sponsorship, and entitlement flags cannot be changed through ordinary profile input. |
| Administration | Broad admin access and role changes could bypass least privilege or remove the last super admin | Explicit role matrix, actor audit records, transactional role changes, and a shared advisory lock protect final-super-admin changes and account deletion. |
| Privacy | Public listings and nested metadata disclosed exact addresses, documents, Wi-Fi/access details; support/chat ownership was weak | Public serialization redacts private fields and reduces coordinate precision. Booking, support, conversation, wallet, payout, and entitlement access is scoped to real participants or allowed roles. Internal support notes remain private. |
| Booking inventory | Concurrent overlap/capacity changes, unsafe room replacement, and calendar imports could corrupt availability | Property row locks serialize reservations and availability changes. Authoritative room capacity, party/date limits, manual/feed provenance, and historical room ID protection are enforced. |
| Pricing | Client identity, totals, fees, options, or discounts could be trusted; floating money inputs were unsafe | Server pricing and commission snapshots, validated catalog options, money precision checks, coupon limits, and atomic wallet debits determine totals. Quotes do not reserve inventory. |
| Checkout | Fake/test checkout, unbound orders, and client-reported payment success | Cashfree intents persist before provider calls. Ownership, purpose, SKU, currency, amount, provider order/payment references, and actual successful receipts are checked. Raw signed webhooks are verified. Legacy simulated checkout is disabled. |
| Database locking | Prisma could not deserialize the void return from pg_advisory_xact_lock | Advisory lock statements use executeRaw; row SELECT locks continue to use queryRaw. The full checkout regression exposed and verified this fix. |
| Late payment | Expired holds could be revived after inventory became available to others | A payment received after a hold expires records the receipt, cancels the reservation, releases its blocks, and schedules its refund. |
| Refunds and settlement | Provider failures/pending refunds could be reported successful, retried with new intent, or lose their confirmed state | Amount reservations cap refunds; stable IDs reconcile uncertain outcomes. Success requires actual confirmation and cannot be downgraded by a stale pending result. Durable reconciliation jobs handle pending receipts. Earnings are held or flagged for recovery. Historical receipt-less refunds are UNKNOWN. |
| Paid entitlements | Host Pro, boosts, and membership could be activated without paid ownership/purpose binding or extended by replay | Paid orders bind owner, product, and target. Activation result persists transactionally; replay does not add another period or bonus. Expired cached activation is reported inactive. Unsupported benefit claims were removed. |
| Loyalty and referrals | Repeated redemption/rewards and moderation changes could create unfunded credits | Wallet and loyalty locks, request keys, paid/completed booking eligibility, and a reward ledger prevent duplicate grants. Moderation reverses and restores the net review reward, including debt when points were already spent. Referral generation uses random collision-resistant codes and owner locks. |
| Messaging and notifications | Spoofed senders, unauthorized conversations, duplicate delivery records, misleading push success | Conversations resolve actual participants. Firebase sockets are rechecked and expire. Message keys and durable notification jobs deduplicate in-app events; push failures stay retryable and promotion opt-out is respected. |
| Email and stay passes | Unsafe HTML, plain credential emails, and external ticket/QR generation exposed data or implied unperformed delivery | Templates escape data and remove invented claims/passwords. SMTP/queue success is explicit. Paid stay passes render locally with bundled fonts and a reservation-reference QR. |
| KYC and remote files | Client-provided flags, shared challenges, plaintext bank/ID data, and unsafe downloads | Owner-scoped provider challenges, encrypted results and bank/ID fields, bounded requests, exact host allowlists, public DNS checks, pinned downloads, and no redirects are used. Missing integrations fail explicitly. |
| Account deletion | Destructive deletes could erase finance history, allow continued access, or leave cloud cleanup untracked | Tombstones preserve financial/history relations, block future access, redact account data, and queue identity/storage cleanup. Upcoming bookings, outstanding earnings, and final-super-admin deletion are blocked. Pending cleanup remains visible. |
| Reports and broadcasts | Synthetic analytics/delivery metrics and nonexecuting work could appear successful | Reports use actual database rows and escaped CSV; only ON_DEMAND report definitions are accepted. Broadcast recipients receive durable jobs, and counts measure created in-app records. Bulk operations are unavailable until a worker exists. |
| Staff accounts | Default/generated passwords, a separate disabled login, and presence-only revocation were misleading | Staff is explicitly a directory; credentials are not issued. Firebase authentication and User.adminRole govern access. Password/session routes return unavailable errors instead of false success. |
| Operational packaging | Embedded credentials/private key/backups, live-message tests, wipe scripts, inconsistent setup | Sensitive artifacts are excluded. Unsafe legacy scripts fail before acting; diagnostics are opt-in counts. Fresh/upgrade migrations, a guarded first-admin CLI, legacy encryption maintenance, UTC database config, nonroot Docker, and setup guidance are included. |
| Dependencies and input | Known vulnerable dependencies, unused SDKs, truthy query-string booleans, permissive date suffixes | Patched lockfile, removal of unused packages, compatible transitive YAML/sprintf overrides, explicit true/false parsing, and strict calendar/timestamp validation. |

## Verification and scope limits

Final results: Prisma schema validation and Nest build passed; 6 unit suites / 48 tests and 23 offline integration checks passed. The checked lockfile reports 0 known npm vulnerabilities. The strict lint snapshot reports 1,899 errors and 167 warnings; it is not a clean lint result.

The exact final results are in `audit/verification-summary.json` and `audit/offline-integration-results.json`. The source inventory records changed, removed, and new files with content hashes. Tests use disposable fixtures and no real provider credentials. No production database, payment, email, bank transfer, deployment, or identity deletion was executed during the audit.

The repair migration backfills existing refund timestamps before enforcing the new column, and both migrations execute inside explicit transactions.

The integration suite loads the complete Nest application, actual Prisma client, and a PostgreSQL-compatible PGlite database. It exercises unauthenticated access, privacy, role boundaries, server pricing, booking replay/overlap, payment confirmation, local ticket rendering, refund retries, support ownership, last-super-admin protection, forged webhooks, booleans, paid membership replay/binding, redemption, review reconciliation, late payment, availability provenance, staff behavior, broadcast opt-out, and migration of existing legacy rows.

PGlite uses a single database connection here. This verifies executable migrations and representative service/HTTP behavior, but does not verify native PostgreSQL multi-worker contention, every deadlock interleaving, or all crash/retry schedules. Firebase and Cashfree responses are mocked. Live KYC response variants, merchant webhook delivery, Firebase security/storage rules, FCM receipts, SMTP delivery, and identity cleanup need staging verification using your configured accounts. Docker configuration was inspected; the image was not built or executed in this environment.

`npm run lint` is not clean. Most remaining findings concern untyped dynamic inputs/results and existing `any` usage under the configured type-aware ESLint rules; additional unused compatibility parameters and style/async findings remain. They are provided with file, line, severity, and rule in `audit/lint-findings.json`; no lint rules were disabled to create a clean result. Compilation and targeted tests do not replace a full typing cleanup or prove every dynamic path correct.

The dependency report describes the checked lockfile on the audit date. Zero known npm advisories at that time does not certify dependencies against unknown or future vulnerabilities. Public Google/Firebase client keys in the retained compiled UI need provider restrictions; these are distinct from removed private merchant/service-account credentials.

## Explicitly unavailable or incomplete features

- Standalone paid experience reservation/cancellation is unavailable until a durable owned reservation, gateway checkout, and refund lifecycle exists. Listings and read-only capacity remain available.
- Wallet top-up and withdrawal are unavailable until payment/settlement integrations exist. Wallet credits require real funded/reward/admin ledger evidence.
- Recurring rent collection and automatic legal PDF generation are unavailable. Uploaded agreements and signatures are owner/tenant scoped; documents must exist before signing.
- Reverse penny-drop is unavailable until provider callbacks and ownership binding are configured.
- Bulk import/mutation is unavailable. Individual reviewed endpoints remain available. Scheduled report workers and report-recipient email dispatch are not implemented; reports are generated on demand.
- Staff directory password reset and force logout are unavailable identity operations. Use Firebase's real identity flows. Directory status/module edits do not grant or revoke User.adminRole.
- The compiled admin frontend may still request legacy credentials or assume older response contracts. Its source must be obtained and updated to Firebase authentication and the new backend behavior.
- Historical financial events and entitlements cannot be proved from the uploaded source or legacy flags. Reconcile original payouts, credits, paid products, and receipts before reopening an upgraded deployment. Partial-refund settlement and completed-payout recovery require reviewed finance handling; they are not silently auto-paid.
- Remote account cleanup can remain pending if Firebase/storage credentials or permissions are missing. Database tombstoning is not proof that every external backup has been erased.

Read SETUP.md for the upgrade pause/reverification procedure, key rotation, legacy encryption, provider configuration, and worker operation. This report identifies boundaries instead of presenting unavailable or untested operations as completed.
